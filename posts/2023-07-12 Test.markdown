A PRACTICE OF MEDITATION
=======================

This is the year of controlled attention. Neville wrote how the goal is to be able to hold impeccable control over mind. He was attempting an on-tap ability to rekindle those magic feelings within. The goal was to be able to call upon them at a moment's notice - to feel them with the unfiltered intensity he first felt them with. The key, he says, is control of attention. This can be honed through the principle of repeated rhythm. It requires consistent and disciplined practice. A daily, repeated effort to slowly ramp up your ability to fix in your minds eye the object of your desire.

The process for achieving this is the implementation of a system that accumulates marginal improvements in attention over the period of practice.
