COMING IN FROM THE COLD
=======================

So this is where I write my posts. One day there was a goat. He was an eager goat. He loved climbing hills and trotting along. One day though, he got stuck. His friends were down the mountain calling his name. A storm was coming. The goat could see it over the opposite hills. He started his way down, but now rocks began to fall. He was in a very precarious position.
