Who am I?
==============

This site serves as a collection of my attempts at investigating human nature. I want to understand how we direct our attention, how we develop discipline, and how the ideas we hold about ourselves find expression in the way we live. The goal is to arrive at a set of principles that I can put into practice, and to share what I learn along the way.

I trained as an architect and have worked on construction projects of varying scales. Much of that work involves communicating an idea that, at first, exists only in your own mind. The vision in your head is never quite the vision in someone else's. Finding the words, drawings and details to make it understood requires patience, clarity & care. Writing here is an extension of that same effort: an attempt to distil the thoughts in my head, the lessons I have learnt, and the questions I have yet to answer.

Dan Holy is a name I write under. I like to keep a little of my life private, while allowing the ideas themselves to be open to examination.

My interest in mindset comes from wanting to close the distance between intention and action. It is easy to feel inspired by an idea; the more interesting question is what becomes of it on an ordinary day. How do we return to the work when enthusiasm fades? How do we remain curious when something challenges us? How do we pursue something meaningful with a little more equanimity and a little less fear of getting it wrong?

These are the questions I hope to explore here, through reading, reflection and practice. Some entries will be considered essays; others may simply be the beginnings of a thought. I expect to revise my views as I learn, and I would like this site to leave room for that.

What this collection will become I am not yet sure. Through a process of continuous improvement, I hope to make it useful to those who find their way here — whether through an idea worth trying, a question worth sitting with, or an appreciation of the process itself.

Thank you for reading. I hope you find something here that stays with you.
